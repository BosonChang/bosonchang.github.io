Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

TODO Pet is a to-do app with a virtual pet.

Without an account

Your pet, tasks and coins are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in

Signing in with Apple or Google lets you sync across your devices. We then store your name (as provided by Apple or Google), a unique account ID, and your app data: pet, tasks, coins and progress.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) and used only to run sync. Your profile photo stays on your device and is not uploaded. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the account screen and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your synced data and account from our servers. Data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what sign-in provides.
