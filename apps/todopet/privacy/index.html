Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Todo Pet is a to-do app with a virtual pet.

Without an account

Your pet, tasks and coins are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in

Signing in with Apple or Google lets you sync across your devices. We then store your name and email address (as provided by Apple or Google), a unique account ID, and your app data: pet, tasks, coins and progress.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) and used only to run sync. If you sign in with Google, your Google profile picture is used as your default avatar on your device. Your profile photo (the Google picture, or one you choose yourself) is saved as a small copy (256 px) with your account so it appears on your other devices; you can change it at any time. If you join a group, a smaller thumbnail of your photo is shared with that group's members. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Subscriptions

Optional paid plans are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. If you use groups, we also store a device push token so we can notify you when a shared task is completed.

Groups

Groups are optional. Group names, messages, shared tasks, and your display name and thumbnail are stored in Firestore and visible to that group's members. You can report a message or block a member from the chat. A report sends the message text, the reported user's ID and your user ID to us for review; the list of people you have blocked is saved with your account so it applies on all your devices. When you delete your account, your messages and completed tasks stay in the group with your name so the history remains readable; your account, sync data and push token are deleted. Owned groups pass to another member.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the account screen and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your synced data and account from our servers. Data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what sign-in provides.
